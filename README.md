# Scubaru – Underwater Taxi


## Game Story

Humans have started living in cities under the ocean. You work as a taxi driver in this world.  

Your task is to pick up passengers and take them to their destinations. Some passengers live in caves, some in nearby cities. You start near the surface where the journey is simple. As you go deeper, the path becomes more complex. You encounter obstacles, enemies, and areas where oxygen is low.  

Each level has three caves and there is a final boss level. You carry passengers while avoiding danger. You can collect coins to buy better equipment. Health kits and oxygen tanks are available if you run low.  

At the end of each level, you receive a score based on time taken, damage received, and passengers delivered. Completing levels safely and quickly or taking risks to carry more passengers both affect your score.  


## How to Play

1. Open the game using the link below.  
2. Move the submarine taxi to pick up passengers.  
3. Deliver passengers to their destinations safely.  
4. Avoid obstacles, mines, and enemies.  
5. Collect coins, health kits, and oxygen tanks along the way.  
6. Complete each level to proceed to the next one.  

**Controls:** Use keyboard or mouse depending on game instructions.
